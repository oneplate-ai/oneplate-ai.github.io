<h1>What Is an LLM?<br />AI Services You Can Use</h1>
<blockquote>Is ChatGPT the only LLM?<br />This time, we will briefly look at what LLM means and at the many AI services and models people can encounter.</blockquote>

<h2>What is an LLM?</h2>
<p><strong>LLM</strong> stands for <strong>Large Language Model</strong>.<br />It is an AI model that learns from a large amount of text and produces answers by predicting what words may come next from the context of a question or sentence.<br /></p>
<p>LLMs can be used to answer questions, summarize text, translate, write, and explain code.<br />They are not people who experience or think about the world; they are closer to tools that compose answers from learned language patterns.<br /><a href="https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/llm/transformers">Read an official introduction to how LLMs work</a><br /></p>
<figure><img src="{{ '/assets/images/easy-ai-03-llm-services-labeled.svg' | relative_url }}" alt="One LLM supporting different kinds of AI services for conversation, search, documents, and code"></figure>

<h2>How are LLMs and services different?</h2>
<p>If an LLM is a <strong>recipe</strong> for preparing food, a service that we use on a website or app is more like a <strong>restaurant</strong> where we can order and receive the food.<br /></p>
<p>Names such as ChatGPT and Gemini usually refer to services that people use directly.<br />A service can use more than one model, and each service can offer different tools and ways of working.<br /></p>

<h2>Representative services you may encounter.</h2>
<p>People can talk with services on the web or in apps such as these.<br /></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>ChatGPT:</strong> OpenAI’s conversational AI service.</li>
<li><strong>Gemini:</strong> Google’s personal AI assistant service.</li>
<li><strong>Claude:</strong> Anthropic’s conversational AI service.</li>
<li><strong>Microsoft Copilot:</strong> Microsoft’s AI companion service.</li>
<li><strong>Perplexity:</strong> An AI search service that provides answers using the web and sources.</li>
<li><strong>Grok:</strong> xAI’s conversational AI service.</li>
</ul>
<p>All of these services let people ask questions and receive answers, but the models they use, the tools they connect to, and the ways they present information can differ.<br />Which service works best depends on the type of question and the situation in which it is used.<br /></p>

<h2>There are also models used by developers and organizations.</h2>
<p>Examples include <strong>Llama</strong>, <strong>Mistral</strong>, <strong>Qwen</strong>, and <strong>DeepSeek</strong>.<br /></p>
<p>These models may be offered through a website where people can chat directly, or they may be connected through an API, cloud platform, or personal computer to support other services.<br />The world of LLMs is not limited to a few chat services; it is a broad ecosystem of models and services working together.<br /></p>

<h2>What should you try first?</h2>
<p>You do not need to learn every service at once.<br />Choose one of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, or Grok and ask it something like, “Explain this idea to a beginner,” or “Summarize the main point of this article in three sentences.”<br /></p>
<p>If the answer is not helpful, you can ask again with a request such as, “Explain it more simply,” or “Add examples.”<br />LLMs can still produce plausible but incorrect answers, so important information about dates, numbers, health, or law should be checked against official sources.<br /></p>

<h2>In one sentence.</h2>
<p class="summary"><strong>An LLM is an AI model that creates answers from language, and it can be used through services such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, and Grok, as well as through model families such as Llama, Mistral, Qwen, and DeepSeek.</strong><br /></p>
